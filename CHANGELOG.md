## [0.1.0](https://github.com/rjach/sambat/tree/v0.1.0) - 2026-10-01

### Calendar data

- The calendar table covers BS 1975–2083. BS 2082 and 2083 are verified against the national panchang of the Nepal Panchanga Nirnayak Bikas Samiti, and BS 2081 against the Surya Panchanga; the other years are cross-checked against open-source tables.

### Features

- First release: `sambat.date` and `sambat.datetime` with full `datetime` parity, the `sambat.calendar` module, Nepali formatting and parsing, BS month arithmetic (`sambat.delta`), periods, Nepal's fiscal year, and the `sambat` command line tool.
